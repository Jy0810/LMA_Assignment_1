Providers of high-risk AI systems must:

1. Ensure compliance with the requirements set out in Section 2.
2. Indicate their name, registered trade name, or registered trade mark, and their contact address, on the high-risk AI system or its packaging or accompanying documentation.
3. Have a quality management system in place that complies with Article 17.
4. Keep the documentation referred to in Article 18.
5. Maintain logs automatically generated by their high-risk AI systems as per Article 19.
6. Undertake the relevant conformity assessment procedure as outlined in Article 43 prior to placing the system on the market or putting it into service.
7. Prepare an EU declaration of conformity according to Article 47.
8. Affix the CE marking if placement on the market or putting into service is involved, as per Article 48.
9. Comply with registration obligations mentioned in Article 49(1).
10. Demonstrate the system's conformity upon a reasoned request from a national competent authority, as per Article 20.
11. Ensure the system meets accessibility requirements as defined in Directives (EU) 2016/2102 and (EU) 2019/882.
12. Take corrective actions and provide requested information as needed.
13. Cooperate closely with new providers when modifying existing high-risk AI systems.
14. Provide necessary information and support to assist new providers in fulfilling their obligations.
15. Notify the initial provider of changes to the AI system's classification status if it was previously determined as non-high-risk.
16. Ensure the system complies with accessibility requirements as defined in Directives (EU) 2016/2102 and (EU) 2019/882.
